FAQ

A few things to know.

Questions about sponsored water campaigns?

Ask us about a campaign
Is the water free for consumers?

That's the FreeSip concept: advertisers fund campaign bottles so recipients can receive them for free.

Is FreeSip a water manufacturer?

FreeSip is designed as a sponsored-hydration advertising business. Production would be arranged with appropriate licensed bottled-water partners.

Can our bottles have a QR code?

Yes. A QR code can connect recipients with a campaign page, offer or other suitable online destination.

Can we choose distribution locations?

You can specify your preferred audience and area. The final plan depends on approvals, partner availability and logistics.

How is a campaign priced?

Pricing is quoted individually based on volume, label specifications, distribution and campaign requirements.

FAQ

What brands usually want to know.

Is FreeSip a water manufacturer?

FreeSip is positioned as a sponsored hydration and brand activation business. Campaign production can be coordinated with suitable licensed bottled-water production partners.

Can a campaign include a QR code?

Yes. A campaign can use QR codes to connect the bottle to a landing page, offer, social profile or other digital destination.

Where can bottles be distributed?

Potential locations include events, universities, offices, gyms and venues, subject to permissions, agreements and logistics.
